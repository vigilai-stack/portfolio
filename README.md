## Projects

**Nilu, an AI-Companion for My Father**  
Designed and built a warm, voice-friendly AI companion that keeps my father company and supports his daily routine after the passing of my mother.

**VigilAI CareIntegrity**  
Institution-wide audit platform for hospitals and health systems.

**Patient CashPay AI Agent**  
An AI front-desk agent that quotes transparent cash prices, books appointments and takes payments, with every refund or exception routed to a human for approval.

**Copay Accumulator Audit**  
Recalculates whether manufacturer copay assistance was credited toward a patient's deductible and out-of-pocket limit, or quietly excluded by the plan, and drafts the appeal letter.

**ProxyAfter**  
Finds patient-portal proxy access that is still active after it should have ended, such as after a death, an 18th birthday, or the end of a guardianship, before it becomes a privacy breach.

**RangeFlip**  
Re-scores trial participants when a site's lab reference ranges change and flags anyone whose eligibility flips, protecting both patient safety and trial integrity.

**OverrideLens**  
Reviews drug-allergy and drug-interaction alert overrides, grading each as justified, weak or unjustified, with the quoted reason and repeat-pattern risk for pharmacy and patient-safety teams.

**WellProof**  
A Pass, Fail or Fix check of a private-well water lab report against accreditation, freshness, completeness and EPA and state limits, so a homebuyer knows what they're buying before closing.

**Med-Sec Audit AI Agent**  
Red, Blue and Green team agents attack, scan EHR access logs and patch; a compliance agent checks findings against HIPAA, HITECH and NIST text.

**Silent Siren**  
A privacy-first safety skill for elders living alone or in senior living.

**QNX Bedside Guardian**  
Alarm escalation for a hospital unit, written in C on QNX 8.0: unanswered high-priority alarms move from bedside nurse to charge nurse to rapid response on fixed deadlines, and a person must acknowledge the exact alarm version they were shown.

**ClearSend**  
Private pre-check for patient portal messages, run on-device in the browser: flags red-flag symptoms ("call 911 now"), strips identifiers a clinic never needs, and routes the message to the right desk.

**TrialGuard**  
An assistant for research teams that reviews protocol deviations, proposes a severity level and corrective actions, and keeps people in charge.

**VigilAI Trial Match**  
Pre-screens patients against a trial's eligibility criteria and marks each one Found, Not found or Unclear, with the evidence behind it.

**Healthcare security set**  
Three tools for healthcare security teams: a security-monitoring detection pack for clinics, a hospital ransomware detector, and a phishing screener for clinic front desks.
